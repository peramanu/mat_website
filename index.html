About

Sound of
the Alps

MĀT is a DJ and producer from South Tyrol, Italy. His music fuses the raw energy of the Alps with modern club sound — authentic, energetic, unforgettable.

Performances at the FIS Ski World Cup Saslong, Kronplatz, Seceda, Club Max and beyond have established him as a powerhouse in the après-ski and club scene.
